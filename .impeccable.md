## Design Context — SmartStock Redesign

### Users
Small canteen / cafe / shop owners in Malaysia (campus canteens first). They are busy, not data analysts. They open the app to get a clear action plan for tomorrow morning — not to explore dashboards. Context: early morning prep, quick decisions, often on a phone or laptop.

### Brand Personality
- **Calm authority** — feels trustworthy and grounded (they're trusting us with money decisions)
- **Organic precision** — natural/food-world warmth, but mathematically precise underneath
- **Quiet confidence** — no panic colors, no shouting; the app knows what to do

Three words: **grounded, fresh, decisive**

### Aesthetic Direction
Codecademy-inspired light theme: warm peach page background (`#FFF5EE`), white cards (`#FFFFFF`), dark navy text (`#10162F`), light gray borders (`#E5E7EB`), golden yellow accent (`#F7C948`), soft blue accents (`#E6F7FF`). Rounded-2xl cards, generous internal padding, soft shadows, floating bottom action bar on mobile, sticky sidebar on desktop.

Theme: **light, friendly, focused**

### Anti-references
- Generic SaaS blue dashboards
- High-contrast neon accents
- Dense spreadsheet-like tables as the main view
- Emojis as icons
- Dark mode default

### Design Principles
1. **Surface first** — white cards float on a warm peach background, depth through soft shadow
2. **Restrained accent** — golden yellow for CTAs, money, and progress; red/amber only for warnings
3. **Generous rounding** — cards, buttons, inputs all rounded-2xl for friendly softness
4. **Data as narrative** — charts and numbers serve a story about tomorrow's plan, not decoration
5. **Touchable** — min 44px tap targets, clear states, reduced-motion respected
